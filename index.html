Transcription Process

  1. RNA polymerase binds to the promoter region
  2. DNA double helix unwinds and separates
  3. RNA polymerase reads the template strand (3' to 5')
  4. Complementary RNA nucleotides are added (5' to 3')
  5. RNA transcript grows as polymerase moves along DNA
  6. DNA re-winds behind the polymerase
