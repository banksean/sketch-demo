What’s happening?

  • RNA polymerase moves along the template DNA strand in the 3′→5′ direction.
  • It builds RNA in the 5′→3′ direction using base pairing rules: A↔U and C↔G (T in DNA pairs with A in RNA).
  • The coding DNA strand matches the RNA sequence except thymine (T) is replaced by uracil (U).
Educational demo. Not modeling promoters or termination; focuses on base pairing and strand orientation.
